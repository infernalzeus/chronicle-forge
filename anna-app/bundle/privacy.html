Privacy & data use

Updated 31 October 2026.

Chronicle Forge runs inside Anna. Research sends your topic to Anna's web search and page extraction services. Drafting sends your topic, selected source excerpts and narration to Anna's configured language-model providers. Image and voice generation send scene prompts and narration to Anna's configured image and speech providers. Those services operate under Anna's privacy policy and their provider policies.

The app does not connect to Ollama, localhost, local image-generation servers or personal API-key files. It does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts or filesystem access. Hosted AI operations use your Anna quota. Browser video encoding uses your device's normal video encoder, not a local AI model.

The app holds your current draft and generated assets in browser memory while its window stays open. It does not add analytics, advertising trackers or its own external database. Anna and its service providers may retain requests and generated artifacts according to their own policies. Media is downloaded from short-lived URLs issued by Anna. Closing or reloading the app loses its in-memory project. Download files you want to keep; downloaded files remain wherever you save them.

Source notes and project exports can contain your narration, prompts and selected public source text. Share them only when you intend to. Generated visuals and speech may be inaccurate; review factual claims and publishing rights yourself. Captions are timed approximately within each speech scene.

For account data, quota, hosted-service retention or deletion requests, use Anna's account and support channels. This app cannot delete provider logs or Anna account data.

Local timing estimates

Chronicle Forge may remember up to twenty completion durations per model and quality setting in this browser, solely to show recent averages. Timing samples contain model/quality labels and elapsed milliseconds, not scripts, prompts, source text or media. No timing data is transmitted automatically. Diagnostics remain an explicit local download.
